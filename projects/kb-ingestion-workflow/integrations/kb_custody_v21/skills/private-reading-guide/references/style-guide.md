# Style Reference: 私人研究型阅读导读

## Desired texture

The prose should feel like a careful researcher writing for themself:

- clear without sounding simplified;
- informed without sounding performatively academic;
- elegant mainly through rhythm and precision, not ornament;
- willing to leave some interpretive space;
- continuous enough to read like an essay rather than an interface.

## High-value transformations

### 1. Replace labels with actions

Less effective:
> 当前存在数据关联不足的问题。

Prefer:
> 数据进入系统以后，并没有自动找到彼此。

### 2. Let judgment arrive later

Less effective:
> 本章最重要的是理解指标如何影响治理。

Prefer:
> 指标可以设计出来，数据也可以收集。但如果结果会改变考核，谁愿意把不好看的数字交出来？到了这里，指标问题已经变成了组织问题。

### 3. Remove the guide standing between reader and material

Less effective:
> 值得观察的是，达成共识、作出承诺、执行承诺为什么不同。

Prefer:
> 达成共识、作出承诺、执行承诺，是三件不同的事。

### 4. Keep two accounts separate only when there are truly two accounts

Useful distinction:
> 社会收益是一笔账，项目现金流是另一笔账。

The value comes from a real structural distinction. Do not manufacture a contrast simply to create rhetorical force.

## Hard constraints for finished prose

### No empty target-setting

Without a real comparison object, avoid constructions that deny a vague alternative before stating the point:

- 不是……而是……
- 要……而不是……
- 并非……而在于……
- 重点不是……而是……
- 真正的问题不是……而是……

State the positive proposition directly.

### No process residue

Finished Human Preview / guide prose should not contain:

- prompt instructions;
- editing history;
- discarded plans;
- implementation notes;
- error reports;
- “已删除……”;
- “这里不讨论……”;
- long self-check sections.

### Consolidate limitations

Do not attach source caveats to every paragraph. Put version limits, missing full text, unread figures, or citation limits into one natural paragraph near the end when possible.

### Numbers stay proportional

A low, partial, or incomplete number should be reported as a state. If it represents a tradeoff, name the tradeoff. Do not escalate one weak metric into a whole-material failure judgment.

### Continuous reading

Prefer natural paragraphs. Use headings only for real changes in thought. Avoid card grids, label walls, one-field-per-line formatting, and excessive blank space.

## Common failure modes

- “本文将从三个维度……” when the source does not require three dimensions.
- “值得注意的是……” repeated as a transition.
- “由此可见……” after the sentence already landed.
- “这充分说明……” without evidence strong enough for the claim.
- “读完自问……” unless exercises were requested.
- “建议重点关注……” repeated across sections.
- multiple paragraphs with identical length and syntax;
- abstract nouns stacked without an actor or action;
- forcing every chapter into a theory;
- turning every source limitation into a disclaimer;
- making a short article pretend to be a book-length study guide.

## Editing heuristic

After drafting, search for:

- 值得
- 建议
- 可以看出
- 由此
- 进一步
- 全面
- 系统
- 有效
- 机制
- 体系
- 关系
- 能力
- 赋能
- 不是
- 而是
- 重点不是
- 真正的问题不是

Do not delete mechanically. Ask whether the sentence becomes clearer if it names an actor, action, object, consequence, or direct positive claim.

## Paragraph test

Each paragraph should do at least one of these:

- add a condition;
- complicate an assumption;
- move from observation to mechanism;
- move from mechanism to implication;
- introduce a real question;
- land on a restrained judgment.

If a paragraph merely repeats the previous paragraph in different words, cut it.

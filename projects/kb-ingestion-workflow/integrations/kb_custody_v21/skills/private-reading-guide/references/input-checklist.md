## Defaults for this user

Unless explicitly changed:

- Reader: self.
- Tone: private research note, restrained and non-teacherly.
- Main body: article-like continuous reading experience.
- Utility material: indexes/maps belong in appendices.
- Cross-reading: preserve meaningful links, do not force them.
- Reading route: light and optional.
- Verification notes: brief and concentrated.
- Ordinary ingestion: source-first, no automatic external fact-check.
- Short papers/articles: no fixed theme count.
- Human Preview / guide: no process residue, no repeated disclaimer paragraphs, no empty contrast templates.
- Kindle: compact Markdown, no wide tables; book guides may keep about 6–8 priority chapters, short sources do not need chapter appendices.
